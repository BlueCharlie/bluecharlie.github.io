Jun 2026 — Jul 2026

Minebea Intec GmbH

Embedded Hardware Engineering Intern

For both my 2025 and 2026 internships, I signed my contracts directly with Minebea Intec GmbH, the company’s German headquarters. I was assigned to product development at the Chengdu office, which operates under the company’s China headquarters in Beijing.

Across my 2025 and 2026 internships, I designed and developed the original PR5170 and its variants, covering PCB hardware, embedded firmware, and desktop software.

  • Designed standard-environment and Ex ec (gas Zone 2) variants of the digital communications mainboard, together with the Ex ec variant of the analog acquisition mainboard.
  • Developed and integrated two Embedded C firmware branches on STC8/8051 controllers: CS5530-based analog acquisition with configurable filtering, and RS485 communication with an external digital weighing platform.
  • Designed and developed TConfig using Electron, React, and TypeScript for multiple weighing instruments, including PR5170, supporting monitoring, parameter configuration, and calibration through RS232/SBI and RS485/Modbus RTU.
  • Used instrument manuals, serial traces, and real-device feedback to correct command formats, data interpretation, and device-state handling across firmware and desktop workflows.

Across my 2025 and 2026 work periods at Minebea Intec, I designed or modified more than 20 PCBs.

A little about me

I’m a third-year Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Waterloo. My engineering work brings together PCB hardware, embedded firmware, and desktop software for industrial instruments.

My projects connect sensor signals and circuit behavior with device communication and software that people use to operate instruments. I use schematics, instrument documentation, serial traces, and hands-on testing to understand problems and refine designs.

I previously provided PCB design services through Charles Tech.
